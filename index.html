HOW TO READ THIS ATLAS

A complete terrain survey

The background is a separate, flat copy of Ironfall’s BlueMap terrain. The original site is unchanged. Live players, claims, markers, and 3D geometry are not copied.

Ore counts come from the supplied world save; the BlueMap imagery was copied on October 25, 2026. Changes made on the live server after the save are not reflected in the ore layers.

Each color uses its own scale: a bright uranium cell and a bright zinc cell mean each is rich for that resource, not that they contain equal quantities. Hide other layers for a clean comparison.

Density is measured at 16 × 16 block resolution: 16 times finer in each direction than the original 256-block cells. Smooth display interpolation blends neighboring measured cells without changing the counts. The grayscale control affects only terrain, keeping ore colors unchanged. Depth is grouped into exact 16-block sections. All-height density sums the entire vertical column. Peak markers rank 256 × 256 areas by ore-block count; example coordinates identify a verified block in a locally rich 16 × 16 × 16 section, not the center of a single vein. No ores are inferred from surface biomes.

Gold and nether gold are separate layers. Amethyst counts solid amethyst and budding amethyst blocks. Tar and bauxite are included as industrial resources.

Terracotta iron markers: the complete map extent was scanned for direct iron-to-plain-terracotta face contacts. Every marked 16 × 16 × 16 section contains at least 80 iron blocks, 16 plain terracotta, 8 tuff, and 8 distinct iron blocks touching terracotta inside that section. A single vein can contain many markers. Nearby markers cluster on screen and separate as you zoom in. This rule can miss small or heavily mined remnants below the thresholds; it does not infer a vein from biome or ordinary iron density.
